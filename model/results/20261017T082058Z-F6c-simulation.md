## Reading

With Recovery, a node that restarts takes part in nothing until it has f + 1 = 2 answers from other nodes, one from the primary of the highest ballot among them. When two of three nodes roll back in one step, only one node can answer. The two nodes cannot complete their recovery from each other. They send no Promise and no Accepted after the rollback. The third node cannot get a majority from new votes. Votes that the two nodes sent before the rollback are still in flight and are valid, because they show the state at the time of the vote. So the expected break of S2 did not occur in the checked states. The cost is liveness: no action of the model ends the recovery of the two nodes, so the cluster decides nothing more. Model L checks liveness; this case is not checked there yet. This is a result that differs from the expectation of plan item 26. It is not proven, because the run is a simulation.
