## Questions

- Step 0a: does the log of a node get shorter while its n_accepted
  stays the same? Property `LogExtendsOwnVotes`. A step that restarts
  the node (crash, wipe, rollback, the end of recovery) is not counted.
- F6c: does a node that rolled back stay in recovery? Invariant
  `RolledBackStaysRecovering`.

## Bounds

- MC_proof_logext_dup: the constants of MC_a_F1_dup (ballot 1, log 1,
  one duplicate) with DecidedIdxMonotone = TRUE.
- MC_proof_logext_b2: as MC_proof_logext_dup with ballot 2, log 2 and
  one loss.
- MC_proof_F6c: the constants of MC_F6c (two of three nodes roll back
  in one step, recovery, ballot 1, log 1).
- MC_proof_F6c_mut: as MC_proof_F6c, with
  `RecoveryComplete <- RecoveryCompleteWithOne` (recovery completes
  with one answer).
