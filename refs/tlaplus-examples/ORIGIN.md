# Origin

The modules MultiPaxosVoting and MultiPaxosVotingProof follow the form of
the voting algorithm of Paxos and its TLAPS proof in tlaplus/Examples.
The source is not copied into this repository. A citation such as
`VoteProof.tla:612` names a file and a line at this commit.

- Upstream repository: https://github.com/tlaplus/Examples
- Commit: 53955cf
- Files: `specifications/byzpaxos/VoteProof.tla` and
  `specifications/byzpaxos/Consensus.tla`
