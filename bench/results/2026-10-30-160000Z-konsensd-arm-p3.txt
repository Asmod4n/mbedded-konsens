Medians of the three rounds (writes/s)
- 1 in flight: P4 369, its copy 390, P3 402.
- 64 in flight: P4 6 351, its copy 6 398, P3 6 437.
- The A/A pair differs by 6 % at 1 in flight and by 1 % at 64 in flight. P3 differs from P4 by 9 % and by 1 %. The 9 % lies above the A/A floor of this run only by 3 points; it is not taken as a gain until it repeats.

Instructions per write (callgrind, 1 in flight)
- R1 with S2: 482 929, 696 972, 560 470 on the three nodes.
- P3 with S2: 364 184, 363 953, 498 454.
- Under callgrind the rate was 93 writes/s for R1 and 130 for P3.

Reading
- P3 removes 18 % to 29 % of the instructions per write. Without callgrind the rate at 1 in flight does not show the same gain. The path of one write at 1 in flight waits for more than the CPU of the nodes. Where it waits is not measured yet.
