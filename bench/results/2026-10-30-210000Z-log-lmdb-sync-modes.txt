Log only: LMDB 1.0.2 (MDB_INTEGERKEY, MDB_APPEND) with MDB_NOMETASYNC and MDB_NOSYNC against SQLite with synchronous=FULL and OFF

What was measured
- bench/log_store.cpp at this commit, the setup of 2026-10-30-205000Z, with a fifth argument for the sync mode. 3 rounds; each cell the median of 31 samples of 192 entries. The second "lmdb nosync" row of each round is the A/A arm.
- strace -c of the default arms (64 per commit, 99 commits, one run): LMDB makes 100 fdatasync and 212 pwrite64; its meta page goes through a descriptor with O_DSYNC, so a commit syncs twice. SQLite makes 106 fdatasync.

Medians over the 3 rounds (entries/s)
| arm | 1 per commit | 64 per commit |
| LMDB default (205000Z) | 624 | 33294 |
| LMDB NOMETASYNC | 1072 | 55353 |
| SQLite FULL | 1249 | 65572 |
| LMDB NOSYNC | 192023 | 2489271 |
| LMDB NOSYNC A/A | 197590 | 2533282 |
| SQLite OFF | 140522 | 1148710 |
- A/A of LMDB NOSYNC: 2.9 % at 1, 1.8 % at 64.
- Without a sync LMDB appends 1.37 times (1) and 2.17 times (64) as many entries as SQLite. With a sync the disk decides; LMDB with its default needs two syncs per commit.
- MDB_NOMETASYNC leaves the meta page of the last commit unsynced until the next commit; it is not the durability of SQLite FULL.
