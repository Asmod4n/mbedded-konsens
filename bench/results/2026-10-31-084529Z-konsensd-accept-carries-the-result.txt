Reading
- At 64 in flight the arm is 16.9 % to 20.2 % faster than the base in all three builds; the A/A differences are 1.9 % or less.
- At 1 in flight the difference is +2.6 % to +10.3 % against A/A differences up to 2.9 %.
- The CPU per write falls by about a third on all builds.
- The arm fails 3 tests of test/test_execute_verify.cpp that check the selection of form 6 (a leader whose result differs, all results differ, a StopSign while a request is in work). Stage 2 of the plan (the selection at a new ballot) is needed before the arm can enter the tree.
