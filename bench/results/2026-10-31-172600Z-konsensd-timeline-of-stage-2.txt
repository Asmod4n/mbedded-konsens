Where the time of one write goes on stage 2, at 64 writes in flight

What was measured
- konsensd at c6d2114 (stage 2 with promise-again-on-the-same-ballot) plus bench/arms/timestamps-of-a-write.patch (sha256 91df7a3746fd8fb5). Build g++-16 Release in localhost/bench-logs with bench-logs/build-konsensd.sh, binary konsensd_g16TSG (sha256 670abe8c6f2db4cc). The pinned sources of mbedded-cbor, slipstreamIO and mruby-webmachine came from local clones through FETCHCONTENT_SOURCE_DIR_*.
- bench-logs/ts-G.sh (sha256 c3eb4e821d1da730): bench/arms/timestamps-of-a-write.sh with cluster2.sh (b5ccbdeaa4a5fac9), so the client is at the leader. 4096 writes of 64 bytes at 64 in flight, three runs. Option sync_by_the_caller = true, Unix sockets. podman --cpus=3, user 1001, not pinned, seccomp profile with io_uring, kernel 6.18.44-fc-v185, 4 vCPU Intel Xeon, three btrfs loop images compress=zstd:1 on one host disk. Run 2026-10-31 17:26 to 17:28 UTC.
- The record costs time, so the rates below are not the rates of the binary without it. The split is the result.

Rates of the runs (the record is on)
- 4026, 4079 and 3914 writes/s; median latency 14716, 14636 and 15195 us. The morning run of stage 2 without the record gave 9467 to 9625 writes/s (2026-10-31-094324Z); the sync times of the host disk were higher in this run (below).

fdatasync, submit to completion, per node (us)
- run 1: p50 2502, 2042, 2356; p90 5324, 3931, 4046.
- run 3: p50 3714, 3515, 3900; p90 6989, 6655, 7075.
- Each node syncs 173 to 227 times in about 1.1 s of writes, 18 to 24 writes per sync of the leader.

The commit of phase one (SQLite commit without the sync, on the loop thread), per node
- p50 43 to 592 us, p90 1152 to 2530 us; sum 306 to 594 ms per node in about 1.1 s of writes.
- Split by whether the own fdatasync of the node is in flight when the commit starts:
  - run 1: in flight p50 555, 463, 51 us; not in flight p50 89, 67, 53 us.
  - run 2: in flight p50 455, 417, 65 us; not in flight p50 78, 60, 45 us.
  - run 3: in flight p50 698, 714, 1391 us; not in flight p50 43, 46, 129 us.
  - The p90 is 0.9 to 2.8 ms in both cases.
- One window of node 1 (run 3): sync end at 10596 us, accept_decide received at 10831 to 10970 us, commit phase one from 11669 to 15151 us (3482 us), accepted sent at 15154 us. The accepted of a follower leaves at the end of the commit of the step that checks the entries.

The commit of phase one against the entries of the step (followers; entries counted from the accept_decide that the leader sent to that follower since the last commit, in order)
- 0 entries: p50 55 to 123 us. 1 to 9: p50 436 to 722 us. 10 to 29: p50 1191 to 1310 us. 30 and more: p50 1553 to 2126 us.
- commit_phase_one of node.hpp holds the whole step: propose_before_the_step, end_step (the check and the apply of the entries) and the commit of SQLite. The time grows with the entries of the step, about 40 to 70 us for each entry with the record on.

Result
- The step of a follower takes about 40 to 70 us of CPU for each entry, on the loop thread, before the node sends its accepted. While a sync is in flight, more entries wait, so the next step is longer. The first reading, "the commit of SQLite waits behind the sync", is wrong: a test in one file outside the tree (SQLite WAL, 20 rows of 64 bytes per commit, while a second thread runs fdatasync on the WAL) gave on ext4 p50 32 to 40 us during the sync and 30 to 32 us without, and on btrfs compress=zstd:1 p50 64 to 167 us during and 56 to 58 us without.
- Not checked: the split of the 40 to 70 us per entry between the check run, the apply and SQLite.
