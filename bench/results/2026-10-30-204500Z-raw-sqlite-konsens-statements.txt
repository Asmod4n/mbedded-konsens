Raw SQLite: one INSERT per write against the statements of a konsensd node per write, 12 and 64 writes per commit

What was measured
- bench/raw_konsens.cpp runs per write the statements that bench/results/2026-10-30-203500Z-konsensd-sql-statements.txt counted on the leader: log INSERT, 31 rows of a range read, 3 point reads of the log, session read and upsert, data read and upsert, log UPDATE; per commit 5 meta reads and 3 meta upserts; the schema of persistent_storage. Record sizes are guessed (64 B value plus 40 B, plus 16 B of result); they are not read from konsensd.
- raw_sqlite.cpp (scratchpad) runs one INSERT into a WITHOUT ROWID table per write.
- Both built in the container with g++-16 -O2 against the distro libsqlite3; WAL, synchronous=FULL, a passive checkpoint at 1000 frames. One process, one btrfs loop image compress=zstd:1, podman --cpus=3, user 1001.
- 3 rounds, each cell the median of 31 samples of 192 writes. No A/A run: the noise floor of this pair is not known, and raw_sqlite at 12 per commit spreads from 4548 to 11322 between rounds.
