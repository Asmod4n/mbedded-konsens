Log only: LMDB 1.0.2 with MDB_INTEGERKEY and MDB_APPEND against SQLite with an INTEGER PRIMARY KEY table, 1, 12 and 64 entries per commit

What was measured
- bench/log_store.cpp appends entries of 104 B (64 B value plus 40 B) with rising keys and commits after each batch. Nothing else is written.
- LMDB 1.0.2 (deps/lmdb of branch lmdb-submodule, mdb.c and midl.c compiled with gcc-16 -O2 into the binary), default flags: each commit syncs. One file, MDB_NOSUBDIR, map 1 GiB.
- SQLite: distro libsqlite3, WAL, synchronous=FULL, passive checkpoint at 1000 frames, BEGIN IMMEDIATE.
- lmdb_copy is the same LMDB arm run a second time in each round (A/A).
- One process, one btrfs loop image compress=zstd:1, podman --cpus=3, user 1001, kernel 6.18.44-fc-v185. 5 rounds; each cell is the median of 31 samples of 192 entries.

Medians over the 5 rounds (entries/s)
| per commit | LMDB | LMDB again (A/A) | SQLite |
| 1 | 624 | 625 | 1224 |
| 12 | 6625 | 5989 | 13571 |
| 64 | 33294 | 32985 | 75615 |
- A/A: 0.2 % at 1, 10.6 % at 12, 0.9 % at 64.
- SQLite appends 1.96 times (1), 2.05 times (12) and 2.27 times (64) as many entries per second as LMDB.
- Cause not measured. LMDB syncs the data pages and then the meta page in each commit; the WAL of SQLite needs one sync (not counted here).
