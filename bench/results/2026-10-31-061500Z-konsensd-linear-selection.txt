Reading
- The leader makes a quarter fewer instructions per write.
- The rate of writes changes by less than or about the noise floor: -5.3 % with g++ 16, +5.9 % with g++ 15, +3.6 % with clang 22, against an A/A of 3.4 %. The write path at 64 in flight is bound by the syncs, not by the CPU of the leader.
