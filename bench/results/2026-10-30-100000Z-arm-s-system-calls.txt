What was measured
- System calls per node at 64 in flight, 4096 writes, for D1 and S (strace -f -c, io_uring_enter, fdatasync, pwrite64, pread64). strace slows the run; the rates are not results. One run of each.
- Binaries and settings as in 2026-10-30-094500Z-konsensd-arm-s.txt. Script count.sh outside the tree (31349ce6cd7a22b8).

Reading
- S makes about 1.7 times the pwrite64 and 1.6 to 2 times the pread64 of D1 per write. The loop of S does not wait for the disk, so it commits more often with fewer writes in each commit.
