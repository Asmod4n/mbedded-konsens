What was measured
- Arm U (plan section 20): konsensd on Unix sockets against TCP over loopback. One binary from 575ec08 (2d99e89f24c13d91), g++ 16.2.0 Release (build-konsensd.sh), copied three times so that each arm is its own file: konsensd_g16US2 on TCP, konsensd_g16US2u on Unix sockets, konsensd_g16US2uc on Unix sockets (A/A). Option sync_by_the_caller on in all three.
- The driver and the nodes use the same address form in one cell: cluster.sh with UNIX=1 writes unix:<run directory>/p<i>.sock and c<i>.sock. driver rebuilt with a unix: dialer (main.go 196053b6f56a6ad6).
- Same bed, settings, samples, container and seccomp profile as 2026-10-30-160000Z-konsensd-arm-p3.txt. arms.sh ecc38295cb6529ee (sets UNIX=1 for arm names that end in u or uc), cluster.sh 29a3cc623c3f1da9. The CPU columns are left out; they read 0 for names longer than 15 bytes.

Medians of the three rounds (writes/s)
- 1 in flight: TCP 421, Unix 420, Unix copy 451.
- 64 in flight: TCP 6 190, Unix 7 085, Unix copy 7 656.
- The A/A pair on Unix sockets differs by 7 % at 1 in flight and by 8 % at 64 in flight.
- At 1 in flight no difference between TCP and Unix sockets is shown.
- At 64 in flight Unix sockets are 14 % (7 085) and 24 % (7 656) above TCP. In rounds 1 and 2 the lowest sample on Unix sockets lies above the median on TCP.
- Multishot is not part of this arm. Accept runs about 64 times per cell, so this bench does not measure the accept path.
