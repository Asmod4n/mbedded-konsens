konsens_bench_aa, the A/A pair) against konsens_bench of the old commit
73d1c67 (konsens_bench_old), which makes one commit per message. The
cases are those of bench/konsens_bench.cpp as each commit has them; the

cases below, through a script outside the tree. Each process runs one
case with one payload: one warm-up sample, then 31 samples; 7 rounds,
the order of the cases and of the binaries turned around each second
round. Each process runs as the user bench (uid 1001) through setpriv,
not pinned, nice 0.

with 1024 B; write_lmdb and write_batch_lmdb with 65536 B; read_lmdb.
