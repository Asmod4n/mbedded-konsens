konsensd: arm X (begin_the_base replays nothing) against 1011ddc, stopped after two rounds

What was measured
- The aim: an upper bound of the cost of the replay in begin_the_base, at 1 and at 64 writes in flight.
- konsensd writes of 64 B through PUT /kv, three nodes, Unix sockets, sync_by_the_caller = true.
- Arms, each its own binary, g++ 16.2.0 Release: konsensd_g16HS2u (1011ddc), konsensd_g16XS2u (1011ddc with begin_the_base returning an empty nested transaction), konsensd_g16HS2uc (the H binary copied, A/A).
- podman --cpus=3, user 1001, not pinned, seccomp profile with io_uring, kernel 6.18.44-fc-v185, btrfs loop images compress=zstd:1, libsqlite3 3.53.4.
- Binaries: konsensd_g16HS2u e4a23155a4362697; konsensd_g16XS2u d1eb8a76bb0cad68. Script outside the tree: arms.sh 0aad1710ea6c3d47.

Result
- Arm X gives no valid number. In round 1 at 1 in flight it answered (319 writes/s). At 64 in flight and in every later cell it got no answer.
- At 1 in flight no own result waits above applied_idx, so the replay is empty there and arm X computes the same results.
- Node 1 of arm X logged: "a majority refused a done record of this leader: A chosen result fails its CRC on this node, or a value that the node wrote reads back different".
- The cause: run_the_tasks and run_again execute a request on the base. Without the replay, the base lacks the done results above applied_idx. A key_value result depends on the earlier writes of the same key, so the followers compute other bytes and refuse.
- Removing the replay thus changes the results. It is not a valid arm, and the replay has no upper bound from this form.
- The run was stopped after round 2. The rows of H and its copy below are not enough for a comparison.
