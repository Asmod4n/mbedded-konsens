What was measured
- Size, encode and decode of one accept_decide message with 1 and 16 entries, as in 2026-10-30-120000Z-cbor-packed-against-schema-and-structs.txt.
- mbedded-cbor fe7eaf2. Test file outside the tree: c.cpp 9134f64c7e50074c.
- Arms, each its own binary, -O2 -DNDEBUG: structs (g++ 16, -std=c++26 -freflection); packed and packed_aa (g++ 16, -std=c++23, description inside; packed_aa a copy, A/A); apart (g++ 16, -std=c++23, cbor::description_place::apart).
- Each value is the median of 15 samples of 20000 iterations, ns per message. 3 rounds, arms alternate.
- podman image localhost/cxx-matrix-sqlite, not pinned, kernel 6.18.44-fc-v185, Intel Xeon (Firecracker VM).
