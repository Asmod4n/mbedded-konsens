Writes per second of konsensd when a step ends after a fixed number of entries

What was measured
- konsensd at c727402 plus bench/arms/cap-of-the-step.patch (sha256 efa06382742b959c, committed in 7a07e96). The patch ends the step of the leader after K client requests, and the step of a follower after each accept_decide. K is a compile-time value: 0 (no cap, the base), 4, 8 and 16. Each K is its own binary. The base of g++-16 is built twice (C0 and C0b) for the A/A comparison; both builds have sha256 68c4368fddaa896c.
- Builds: Release, g++-16, g++-15 and clang++-22 in localhost/bench-logs with bench-logs/build-konsensd.sh. Base binaries sha256: g16 68c4368fddaa896c, g15 d4220a4698611b48, c22 088b67293940ce02. Pinned sources of mbedded-cbor, slipstreamIO and mruby-webmachine from local clones through FETCHCONTENT_SOURCE_DIR_*.
- bench/arms/cap-of-the-step.sh (sha256 c4ec09fddad70331) with cluster2.sh (b5ccbdeaa4a5fac9): three nodes, client at the leader, sync_by_the_caller = true, Unix sockets. Driver sha256 f0ea99b7dcc29b95: 3 samples of 300 writes at 1 in flight and of 8192 writes at 64 in flight, 64 bytes each. Four rounds, the arms in turn inside each round.
- podman --cpus=3, user 1001, not pinned, seccomp profile with io_uring, kernel 6.18.44-fc-v185, 4 vCPU Intel Xeon, three btrfs loop images compress=zstd:1 on one host disk. Run 2026-10-31 18:00 to 18:30 UTC.
- The value per round is the median of its 3 samples. The table gives the median, the minimum and the maximum over the 4 rounds. cpu = user + sys us per write, summed over the three nodes.

Noise
- A/A at 64 in flight: g16C0 10002 against g16C0b 10097 writes/s, 0.9 %. The spread inside one arm over the 4 rounds is up to 42 % (g16C0: 9598 to 13582).
- A/A at 1 in flight: g16C0 481 against g16C0b 544 writes/s, 12 %. 

Result
- At 64 in flight, no cap is faster than the base above the noise in the table as a whole. K = 4 and K = 8 are 2 to 9 % below the base in all three builds. K = 16 is 11 % above the base with g++-16, 1 % below with g++-15 and 5 % below with clang++-22. All of these differences are inside the spread of the rounds.
- At 1 in flight, a step holds one entry, so the cap does not act. The differences are inside the A/A difference.
- A smaller step does not make konsensd faster. The cost of each entry stays the same, and the steps only become more.
- Not checked: the number of entries per step in the capped arms. The patch was not traced.
