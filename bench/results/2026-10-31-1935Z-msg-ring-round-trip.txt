The time of one round of MSG_RING jobs to a pool thread and back

What was measured
- One file outside the tree (sha256 67a5ce904bd7fb4a): the main thread sends one job by IORING_OP_MSG_RING with IOSQE_CQE_SKIP_SUCCESS to each of n pool threads, and waits with io_uring_wait_cqe until n answers came back. Each pool thread has its own ring and waits with io_uring_wait_cqe. With read=true each pool thread runs BEGIN, one SELECT of a 64-byte BLOB by INTEGER PRIMARY KEY, and COMMIT on its own read-only connection (SQLITE_OPEN_READONLY | SQLITE_OPEN_NOMUTEX) of a WAL file, while a writing connection stays open. 20000 rounds per run, the time of each round by steady_clock.
- liburing and slipstreamIO of the konsensd build (kbuild/g16C0), libsqlite3 3.53.4 of the image, -O2. g++-16, g++-15 and clang++-22 in localhost/bench-logs. podman --cpus=3, user 1001, not pinned, seccomp profile with io_uring, kernel 6.18.44-fc-v185, 4 vCPU Intel Xeon. The file on btrfs compress=zstd:1. 4 repetitions, the build and the mode in turn. Run 2026-10-31 19:30 UTC.

Result
- One round to one pool thread and back takes 23.8 to 24.3 us (median of the 4 p50), to two pool threads 30.0 to 31.4 us. The read transaction with one SELECT adds about 1 us. The three builds agree within 1 us.
- A step that uses the pool pays this time once. Not checked: a form that does not sleep in io_uring_wait_cqe.
