Where the instructions of a write go, parts A, B and C, 64 in flight

What was measured
- callgrind (valgrind 3.27.1) on each of the three nodes of konsensd_g16ABCS2u (3a64734, sha256 97d724aa303c5dfb), g++-16 Release. Option sync_by_the_caller = true, Unix sockets, data on tmpfs. bench-logs/vg13.sh (sha256 02c458ce08bc8b81): 200 writes at 1 in flight to warm up, counters set to zero, then 4096 writes of 64 bytes at 64 in flight (dump c64). Leader: node 3. Counts of instructions (Ir), not time. One repetition.

Totals for the 4096 writes
- leader n3: 1 168 586 358 Ir, 285 000 per write
- follower n1: 719 589 235 Ir, 176 000 per write
- follower n2: 738 070 742 Ir, 180 000 per write

Leader, inclusive share of the total
- execute_verify 20.7 %
- sqlite3_step 20.0 %
- check_the_done_records 15.6 %
- selection::select_results 15.3 %, of it find_the_counted_nodes 5.0 % (counts 4.8 %) and holds 3.5 %
- run_the_tasks 8.9 %
- apply_the_done_records 5.9 %
- selection::count_answer 4.7 %
- selection::find_the_done_records 3.9 %
- selection::move_pointer 2.9 %
- decode of request_result (cbor generic_read) 5.0 %

Follower n1, inclusive share of the total
- execute_verify 35.4 %
- sqlite3_step 30.3 %
- run_the_tasks 29.5 %
- check_the_done_records 22.1 %
- decode of request_result 7.7 %

Calls on the leader
- crc32c::Extend 1 035 221 calls (253 per write); 782 032 of them from compute_result_crc. compute_result_crc has 391 016 calls (95 per write): count_answer 128 542, select_results 122 789, holds 122 789.
- select_results 6 973 calls; it calls holds and find_the_counted_nodes for each open entry from pointer_ + 1 on. holds and counts each walk again from pointer_ + 1 to the entry and compute the result CRC of each entry on the way (src/selection.cpp, holds, counts). The work of one call grows with the square of the open entries.
- operator new 991 964 calls (242 per write); std::_Rb_tree_increment 1 763 897 calls.

Reading
- On the leader, the selection of results (select_results, count_answer, find_the_done_records, move_pointer) is 26.8 % of the instructions. Most of it is the repeated walk from pointer_ in holds and counts.
- check_the_done_records (15.6 % on the leader) is read_the_open_log 5.3 %, write_the_done_results 3.9 % (of it decode_result 2.6 %), check_the_done_record 3.7 % and begin_step 2.2 %. decode_result has 8 068 calls there, about 2 per write. It is not a repeated decode of the whole window.
