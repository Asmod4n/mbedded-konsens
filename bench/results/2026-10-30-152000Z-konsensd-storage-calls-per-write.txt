Reading
- At 64 in flight, write_the_result and the session writes run about 4.6 times for each write.
- At 1 in flight, get_meta runs about 36 times and get_entries about 13 times for each write. 5 transactions begin for each write, and 2 of them commit a write.
- The change cuts read_checked_meta from 10.1 to 7.0 calls per write at 1 in flight, and sqlite3_step from 84 to 80. It is not kept.
- Under valgrind the run is slower, so the work that a timer starts (begin_step of a tick) is a larger part of the counts than in a native run.
