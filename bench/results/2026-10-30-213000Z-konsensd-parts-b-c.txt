konsensd: parts B and C of plan-one-read-per-round (state of an entry by decided_idx, read back only in a debug build) against the base, six rounds

What was measured
- konsensd writes of 64 B through PUT /kv, three nodes, Unix sockets, sync_by_the_caller = true.
- Arms, each its own binary, g++ 16 Release: konsensd_g16baseS2u (f2f2fc5), konsensd_g16BCS2u (03a0cd6), konsensd_g16baseS2uc (the base binary copied, A/A).
- Tests of the changed code, green with g++ 16 Release: test_persistent_storage (48), test_sequence_paxos (39), test_execute_verify (32), test_node (12), test_simulation.
- 6 rounds; in each round the arms alternate; each cell is the median of 3 samples (300 writes at 1 in flight, 8192 at 64). Raw SQLite alone on node 1 and three at once, in each round.
- CPU: user and system time of the three konsensd processes together, per write, from /proc/<pid>/stat (arms2.sh 10a7387afd5d5713; arms.sh found no process, because pgrep -x matches 15 characters only).
- podman --cpus=3, user 1001, not pinned, seccomp profile with io_uring, kernel 6.18.44-fc-v185, three btrfs loop images compress=zstd:1 on one host disk.
- Binaries: base 2a4fd395a8a556ad; B+C 269c6dbb4c0455ef; copy 2a4fd395a8a556ad.
- Only g++ 16 was built; g++ 15 and clang 22 are not in this table.
