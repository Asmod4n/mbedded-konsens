konsensd: instructions and SQLite statements per write, by callgrind

What was measured
- konsensd 1011ddc, g++ 16 Release (build-konsensd.sh), binary konsensd_g16HS2u e4a23155a4362697. Three nodes, Unix sockets, sync_by_the_caller = true, election_ticks = 100.
- Each node runs under valgrind --tool=callgrind (image localhost/bench-vg). The data directories are tmpfs, so the counts hold the CPU work and no wait on a disk.
- 1024 writes of 64 B at 1 in flight are counted after a warm run of 1024; then 4096 writes of 64 B at 64 in flight. callgrind_control resets the counts before each part and dumps them after it.
- Script outside the tree: vg7.sh 741cffa7b6a697a7, vgwrap.sh 3b77eb20dcdb4533.
