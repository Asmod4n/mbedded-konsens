Result
- The rate of one cluster falls with each sample, for D1 and for S2. The -wal file and the page cache are not the cause.
- On the leader, the work that grows is the rebuild of the base for each new task: the results that wait above log_len are written again each time. Their number and the steps per write grow from sample 1 to sample 9. Why the queue grows is not measured.
