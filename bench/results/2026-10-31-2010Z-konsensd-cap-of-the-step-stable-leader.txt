Noise
- A/A: g16C0 13955 against g16C0b 13877 writes/s, 0.6 %. The rounds of one arm spread up to 14 % (g16C0b 12530 to 14292); most arms spread under 8 %.

Result
- A cap on the entries of a step makes konsensd slower in all three builds: K = 4 by 30 to 32 %, K = 8 by 16 to 18 %, K = 16 by 6 to 10 %. The cpu per write grows as the step gets smaller.
- A fit of cpu = a + b / K to K = 4 and K = 8 gives a = 99 us per write (all three nodes) and b = 184 us per step. The base, with no cap, uses 98 to 103 us per write. So in the base the cost of a step is spread over many entries, and the cost per write is about the cost of the entries alone. The fit is arithmetic on two points, not a measurement of a and b.
- The run of 1830Z showed no difference because the client wrote to a follower and an election happened during the run.
