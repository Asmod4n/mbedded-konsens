konsensd: part C of plan section 26 (entries of the log from memory) against part A, six rounds

What was measured
- konsensd writes of 64 B through PUT /kv, three nodes, Unix sockets, sync_by_the_caller = true.
- Arms, each its own binary, g++ 16 Release: konsensd_g16A26S2u (write-path 35a6b5d, part A), konsensd_g16C26S2u (6e5f088 with bench/arms/plan-26-part-c.patch), konsensd_g16A26S2uc (the A binary copied, A/A).
- Part C keeps up to 4096 entries of the log in memory after their commit, and transaction::read_entries answers from memory when every key of the range is there. It is an arm only.
- Tests of the changed code, green: test_persistent_storage, test_node, test_execute_verify, test_simulation.
- 6 rounds; in each round the arms alternate; each cell is the median of 3 samples (300 writes at 1 in flight, 8192 at 64). Raw SQLite alone on node 1 and three at once, in each round.
- podman --cpus=3, user 1001, not pinned, seccomp profile with io_uring, kernel 6.18.44-fc-v185, three btrfs loop images compress=zstd:1 on one host disk, libsqlite3 3.53.4.
- Binaries: konsensd_g16A26S2u 2a4fd395a8a556ad; konsensd_g16C26S2u e7b879895010dd18; konsensd_g16A26S2uc 2a4fd395a8a556ad. Patch 14d77a45769369fe. Script outside the tree: arms.sh 66ce544824bc648a.
